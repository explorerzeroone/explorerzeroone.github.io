How it started

It started when I was in 8th grade. I started to notice moments by slowing down and wanted to capture those very moments. I eventually ended up just as interested in what a scene looks like as in how it works. Photography started as a way to slow down and pay attention and eventually turned into a way of telling a story and a show of how I think.


When I'm not behind a camera, I'm usually building something — may it be food, code, or even problems, whatever teaches me something new — or planning the next place I want to explore, ideally on two wheels. I capture photos and videos because I like to, and wish to become a creator someday.
