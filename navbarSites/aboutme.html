About

"Life isn't something to watch from the sidelines. It's something to explore, understand, and create."

A curious engineer, creative storyteller, and lifelong explorer — driven by freedom, learning, and the pursuit of meaningful experiences.

I'm an engineering student, but I don't define myself by my degree. I'm someone who stays endlessly curious about how the world works — physics, computers, photography, music, philosophy, machines, people. I'd rather understand something from first principles than take it on faith.

I don't like living on autopilot. I care more about freedom, exploration and genuine experience than I do about status or prestige. I'm analytical by nature, but just as much a creative one — I don't think logic and art are opposites. I think they explain each other.

What I hold onto

  • Freedom over comfort
  • Curiosity over certainty
  • Growth over perfection
  • Authenticity over appearance
  • Meaning over popularity
  • Experiences over possessions
  • Independence, without letting go of the people who matter

What fills my time

Engineering & Technology

I like learning how things work from first principles — computers, physics, and the harder engineering problems in between. Right now that means AI, robotics, space technology and automotive engineering.

Photography

My favourite creative outlet. Landscapes, travel, portraits — storytelling through images. I'd rather catch a genuine moment in natural light than stage a perfect one.

Music

Self-taught guitarist, mostly acoustic and rock. Slowly starting to write my own songs — usually about freedom, hope and figuring myself out.

Books

I gravitate toward the philosophical and psychological — books that make me see the world differently, not just ones that pass the time.

Travel

Not about luxury. I dream of riding a motorcycle across remote mountains, forests and unfamiliar countries — meeting people, seeing how they live, coming back different.

How people would describe me

What moves me

I want to become someone capable of building meaningful things — software, music, photographs, engineering projects. Work that creates real value while still letting me live freely.

Success, to me, isn't a title. It's having enough independence to choose what I work on, and where I wake up tomorrow.

Somewhere down the road

  • Build useful technology
  • Keep learning, indefinitely
  • Travel extensively, mostly by motorcycle
  • Make music
  • Document the journey through photographs
  • Meet people who see the world differently than I do
